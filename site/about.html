The Furnist 365

Home, Office, and Hospital Solution

The Furnist 365 designs and manufactures furniture for the way Bangladesh actually lives and works — from a single bedroom set to a fully fitted office floor, a hospital ward or a factory line.

Our range spans living, bedroom and dining furniture for homes, complete office solutions in wood and steel, and specialised institutional, hospital and industrial products. Every series shares the same finishing standard, so individual pieces can be combined into a matching set.

What We Do

Six collections covering every corner of a home or workplace.

Materials that hold up

Seasoned hardwood frames, engineered wood panels and steel where strength matters. Lacquer and matte finishes are applied in-house.

Series-based design

Popular, Premium and Flagship series each keep a consistent design language, so a bed, wardrobe and dressing table bought together actually match.

Delivered and installed

We deliver nationwide, and inside Dhaka our own team handles assembly so the piece arrives ready to use.

Segments we serve

  • Home — bedroom, living and dining furniture across four bed sizes and one- to four-door cupboards.
  • Office — director, executive, conference and reception tables; swivel, high-back and visitor chairs; workstations and steel storage.
  • Academic — lab tables, podiums, auditorium chairs and classroom desks.
  • Hospital — ICU and patient beds, examination beds, trolleys, bedside lockers and medicine cupboards.
  • Industrial — QC and packing tables, heavy-duty and slotted angle racks, personal lockers and fire doors.

Interior projects

Beyond catalogue products we take on interior work — kitchen cabinets, functional built-in furniture and complete project fit-outs. If you have a floor plan, our team can work from it.
